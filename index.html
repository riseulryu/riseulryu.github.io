Approach

  • Constructive alignment of outcomes, activities and assessment
  • Active learning: flipped classroom, live demonstration, applied tasks every session, multi-week projects
  • Assessment for generative AI: a few secured points (supervised work, demonstration, oral account of submitted code), with other tasks open to AI use as an object of study
  • Timely, rubric-aligned feedback; active presence in Canvas and Brightspace
  • Early support for at-risk students, with Learning Support Coordinators
  • Experience with international and culturally diverse cohorts since 2019
